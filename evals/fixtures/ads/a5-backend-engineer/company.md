## Software for the people who keep Europe's freight moving

Tessel Cargo Software was founded in 2017 in Antwerp by two former operations managers, Margot Vandenberg and Piotr Lis, who were tired of tracking shipments in spreadsheets. Today more than 340 freight forwarders in 19 countries use Tessel to plan routes, book carriers and give their own customers live shipment tracking.

**Our products**
- **Tessel Plan** – route and load planning with constraint-based optimisation
- **Tessel Track** – a live tracking API and customer portal fed by carrier events (EDI, telematics, webhooks)
- **Tessel Docs** – digital delivery notes and customs paperwork

**Numbers (2026)**
- 120 employees across Antwerp, Poznań and a remote-first engineering group
- 38 million shipment events processed per day
- Series B funding of EUR 24 million closed in March 2026, led by a Belgian growth fund

**How we work**
We keep teams small and own what we run. Engineers carry a pager for their own services, write short design notes instead of long specs, and spend one day per sprint on reliability work. We publish an engineering blog and sponsor the local Python meetup.

**Values:** Be dependable. Say what you see. Leave it better.

## Tessel Cargo Software raises EUR 24 million to expand live tracking across Europe
**Antwerp, 12 March 2026**

Tessel Cargo Software, the Antwerp-based provider of routing and tracking software for freight forwarders, announced a EUR 24 million Series B round. The funding will be used to double the engineering team by the end of 2027, extend the Tessel Track API with predictive ETAs and open a second engineering hub in Poznań.

"Forwarders still lose hours every day to chasing status updates," said co-founder and CEO Margot Vandenberg. "Our goal is that a dispatcher never has to phone a driver to ask where a trailer is."

CTO Piotr Lis said the company is migrating its event pipeline from queue-based workers to Kafka streams to support peak loads during the autumn peak season, and is investing in observability and on-call tooling. "We want engineers who enjoy running the things they build," he added.

Tessel says it processed 38 million shipment events per day in February 2026, up 2.4x from a year earlier, and that its tracking API maintained 99.95% availability over the last twelve months.

## Build the software behind Europe's supply chains

**Engineering at Tessel**
- Stack: Python (FastAPI), Go for a few high-throughput workers, PostgreSQL, Kafka, AWS (EKS, RDS, S3), Terraform, GitHub Actions, Grafana/Prometheus/OpenTelemetry
- Team structure: product teams of 6-8 engineers with an engineering manager and a product manager
- Practices: trunk-based development, code review within one working day, blameless post-mortems, one week in six on call (with a rota bonus)
- We publish our architectural decisions as short ADRs and invite candidates to read two of them before the interview.

**Benefits:** hybrid work, EUR 1,500 learning budget, 28 days holiday, pension scheme, home-office allowance, company-paid language lessons.

**Hiring process:** intro call (30 min) -> technical conversation about a past project (60 min) -> system-design discussion (60 min) -> meeting with the team and engineering manager. No take-home tests.

**What we look for:** people who have run software in production, communicate clearly in writing, and like understanding the business problem behind a ticket. Logistics experience is a bonus, not a requirement.

**Open roles:** Backend Engineer (Python) – Routing & Tracking · Senior Data Engineer · Site Reliability Engineer · Product Designer · Customer Success Manager (Nordics)
