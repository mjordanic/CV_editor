# About FreshCart Supermarkets
*(fictional web page – freshcart.example/about)*

FreshCart is a Croatian supermarket chain founded in 2008 in Velika Gorica. It runs 46 neighbourhood supermarkets in Zagreb, Zagreb County and Karlovac County and employs about 1,900 people.

Every FreshCart store larger than 800 m² has a staffed meat and deli counter. Fresh meat comes from Croatian farms through the company's own distribution centre in Velika Gorica; deliveries reach the stores on pallets every morning before 07:00.

**What we stand for:** fresh local food, fair prices, and staff who know their products. "Ask the counter" is our promise: customers should get honest advice on cuts and cooking from trained people.

**Company facts (2026)**
- 46 stores, 31 with a service meat counter
- About 60% of fresh meat from Croatian producers
- Distribution centre in Velika Gorica with its own cutting room

# Working at FreshCart
*(fictional careers page – freshcart.example/careers)*

**Store teams:** each meat counter has 3 to 6 people across two shifts, led by a counter lead who reports to the store manager. New colleagues get a two-week introduction with an experienced butcher and a paid HACCP refresher.

**Training:** FreshCart pays for forklift and pallet-truck courses for store staff who handle deliveries, and supports colleagues who prepare for the master craftsman exam (majstorski ispit) with paid exam days.

**Benefits:** meal allowance, 10% staff discount, Christmas bonus, transport allowance for public transport.

**What we look for:** reliability, care for hygiene, friendliness with customers, and pride in good work. Experience matters more to us than diplomas for counter roles.

**How we hire:** a short phone call, a 30-minute meeting at the store and a trial half-day at the counter (paid).
