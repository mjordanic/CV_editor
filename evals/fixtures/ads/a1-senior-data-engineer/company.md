# Halden Pay (fictional)

Halden Pay is a fictional fintech headquartered in Lisbon. It gives small and mid-sized exporters one platform for business accounts, foreign-exchange risk management and trade finance. It has roughly 1,400 staff in about 40 offices and opened a new engineering office in Eastern Europe this year.

**Careers page:** the company says it values working together, owning outcomes and moving quickly. It offers hybrid work, a yearly learning budget and a mentoring programme. Engineering is split into product areas (payments, risk, data platform, client experience).

**Data Platform team:** runs the company's central Google Cloud data platform (BigQuery, Airflow, dbt), which data science, finance, risk and reporting teams use. The team contributes to open-source tools and is currently focused on data quality and governance.
