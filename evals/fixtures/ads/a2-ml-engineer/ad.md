# Machine Learning Engineer – Customer Support AI

**Company:** Quillstone Payments (fictional name; card-payment and business-tools provider)
**Location:** Hamburg, Germany (office-first)
**Team:** AI team within Customer Operations

## About the team

The Customer Operations organisation wants merchants to get help quickly and without friction. The AI team builds models and assistants that answer merchant questions, route tickets and help support agents. You will work with backend engineers, data scientists and product managers to take ideas from prototype to production.

## What you will do

- Build, deploy and operate AI and machine learning systems used by the support organisation
- Maintain pipelines for data processing, training and serving
- Fine-tune and evaluate models, including language models, for accuracy and cost
- Prepare and clean large text datasets
- Apply normal engineering discipline: version control, CI/CD and automated tests
- Integrate ML components into the wider product with other engineering teams

## Requirements

- Degree in machine learning, computer science or a related engineering field
- At least four years of experience as a machine learning engineer shipping ML or AI systems to production
- Experience with AI products such as chat assistants or retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
- Software engineering skills for production systems: model serving, monitoring, data versioning
- Experience with a major cloud provider (AWS, GCP or Azure)
- Strong Python; familiarity with MLOps and orchestration tools such as MLflow, Kubeflow, Airflow or an LLM-observability tool
- Experience with feature engineering and data ingestion pipelines

## Benefits

- Annual learning budget, employee share programme and company pension contribution
- Relocation support and subsidised meals
- Sabbatical after several years of service
