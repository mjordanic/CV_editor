# Quillstone Payments (fictional)

Quillstone Payments is a fictional Hamburg-based fintech founded in 2013. It serves a few million small merchants in Europe and the Americas with payment terminals, a point-of-sale app and a business account, and has recently started offering a personal account to the merchants' customers. About 2,500 people of many nationalities work in some 20 offices.

**Values:** take ownership, put the team first, learn from mistakes.

**Engineering:** product teams own their services end to end. The machine-learning group builds customer-support automation (assistants that answer merchant questions from help-centre content) and fraud models.

**Careers page:** relocation support, a training budget and regular peer feedback.
