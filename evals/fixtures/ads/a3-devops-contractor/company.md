# Corvale Mobility (fictional)

Corvale Mobility is a fictional transport company that runs long-distance buses and a growing passenger-rail service in Central Europe, with technology offices in Kraków and two other cities. It describes itself as an engineering-led company where teams own their systems.

**Rail plans:** Corvale is ordering a new fleet of trains to enter service later this decade. The cloud team in the ad builds the platform that connects the trains' on-board systems (telemetry, video, passenger information) to the company's backend services.

**Working with Corvale:** many technology roles are open to independent contractors as well as employees; the company offers hybrid working and travel benefits on its own network.
