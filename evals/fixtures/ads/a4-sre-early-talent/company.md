# Aurelis Cloud (fictional)

Aurelis Cloud is a fictional cloud provider headquartered in Helsinki, with about 1,200 employees and engineering hubs in several European countries. It sells infrastructure for AI workloads: GPU clusters, managed model inference, storage and networking, to customers from start-ups to large enterprises.

**Engineering culture:** small teams that own their services in production, blameless incident reviews, and an on-call rotation shared by developers and site-reliability engineers.

**Early talent:** a paid programme of three to six months for students and recent graduates, with a mentor for every participant and real production work.
