## Software that makes care feel simple

Verdane Care was founded in 2019 in Graz by physiotherapist Dr. Lena Moser and software engineer Jure Kovač. After watching clinics lose hours to phone calls and missed appointments, they built the first version of the Verdane booking app for a single physiotherapy practice. Today more than 1,900 private clinics, physiotherapy practices and dental offices in Austria, Germany, Croatia and Slovenia use Verdane.

**Products**
- **Verdane Book** – online appointment booking, reminders by SMS and e-mail
- **Verdane Intake** – digital intake and consent forms with encrypted storage
- **Verdane Move** – exercise plans and follow-up messages for physiotherapy patients

**Company facts (2026)**
- 68 employees in Graz and Ljubljana, plus a handful of remote colleagues
- 2.1 million patients reached each year
- ISO 27001 certified since 2024; hosted exclusively in EU data centres
- Customer satisfaction (NPS): 61

**Our principles:** Patients first. Privacy by default. Simple beats clever.

**Accessibility commitment:** Verdane applications are built to WCAG 2.2 AA; we run quarterly audits with the a national accessibility association.

## Verdane Care launches redesigned patient app and opens engineering hub in Ljubljana
**Graz, 22 September 2026**

Verdane Care, the Graz-based provider of appointment-booking software for private clinics, has launched a completely redesigned patient experience built on a new React-based design system. The new app cuts the median booking time from 94 to 41 seconds and passes the WCAG 2.2 AA audit commissioned from an independent accessibility consultancy.

The company also opened a small engineering hub in Ljubljana, Croatia, to recruit developers who prefer working in the Adriatic time zone. "Great engineers do not all live in Graz," said co-founder and CTO Jure Kovač. "We are hiring full-stack engineers who care about accessibility and about the people who will use the software."

Co-founder and CEO Dr. Lena Moser announced that Verdane will release a public developer API for clinic software vendors in spring 2027. The company says all patient data remains in EU data centres and that Verdane was recently re-certified under ISO 27001 without findings.

Verdane employs 68 people and plans to hire eight engineers, two designers and one data protection specialist before mid-2027.
