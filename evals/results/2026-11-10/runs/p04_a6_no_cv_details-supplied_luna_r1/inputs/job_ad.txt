# Full-Stack Engineer (TypeScript / React) – Patient Experience

**Company:** Verdane Care (FICTIONAL)
**Location:** Graz, Austria (hybrid) or fully remote within Austria, Germany, Croatia or Slovenia
**Employment:** Full-time, permanent (or B2B contract in Croatia on request)
**Team:** Patient Experience squad (4 engineers, 1 designer, 1 product manager)

## About the role

Verdane Care makes appointment-booking and follow-up software for private clinics and physiotherapy practices. Patients use our web app and mobile web experience to book visits, fill in intake forms, receive reminders and view exercise plans.

As a Full-Stack Engineer you will build and improve those patient-facing features end to end – from React components and accessibility to the Node.js APIs and the database behind them. You will work in a small squad with a designer and a product manager, and you will ship to production several times a week.

## What you will do

- Build new patient-facing features in React and TypeScript, with a strong focus on accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) and performance on low-end phones
- Implement Node.js (NestJS) APIs and PostgreSQL schemas to support those features
- Integrate with third-party systems such as clinic calendars, SMS providers and payment gateways
- Write unit, integration and end-to-end tests (Jest, Playwright) and keep the build green
- Take part in design reviews, usability sessions and sprint planning
- Handle personal health data responsibly: follow our GDPR and data-minimisation guidelines, support audit requests from clinic customers
- Mentor interns or junior colleagues when the opportunity arises

## What you bring

**Must have**
- 2+ years of professional experience building web applications with React and TypeScript
- Experience writing backend services in Node.js (Express, NestJS or similar) and working with SQL databases
- Understanding of semantic HTML, responsive layouts and web accessibility basics
- Experience with automated testing (unit and end-to-end) and Git-based workflows
- Awareness of web security fundamentals (authentication, OWASP Top 10) and willingness to learn the privacy requirements of health data
- Fluent English; German or Croatian is helpful for customer-facing conversations

**Nice to have**
- Experience with Next.js or other server-side rendering frameworks
- Experience in healthcare, wellness or other regulated environments
- Familiarity with AWS or a similar cloud, and with Docker
- Experience with design systems and Storybook
- Experience with analytics and A/B testing

## What we offer

- Salary range EUR 54,000 – 70,000 gross per year depending on experience (Austrian collective agreement minimum applies)
- 25 days of holiday plus the Austrian public holidays, 5 additional well-being days and a EUR 1,200 yearly education budget
- Flexible hours, a home-office stipend and a yearly team retreat
- A friendly, low-ego team that cares about the people who use our software
