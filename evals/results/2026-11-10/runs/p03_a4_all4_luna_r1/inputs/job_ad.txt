# Site Reliability Engineer – Early Talent Programme

**Company:** Aurelis Cloud (fictional name; AI-focused cloud infrastructure provider)
**Location:** Helsinki, Finland
**Programme:** paid, 4–6 months, starting March 2027
**Eligible:** current students (computer science or related), recent graduates and early-career engineers with the right to work in Finland

## About the programme

You will join the Site Reliability team that keeps the company's cloud and GPU infrastructure running, with mentoring from experienced engineers and exposure to real customer workloads. A full-time offer may follow.

## What you will do

- Help with the team's daily operational work
- Complete tickets from the team backlog or run a small, well-defined project of your own
- Write tests for your changes and document your work
- Keep tasks up to date in the team's issue tracker
- Study Linux system fundamentals (networking, resource isolation, tracing)
- Learn the team's tools and standards, plus Kubernetes, Helm and Terraform
- Learn the basics of configuration management and automation

## What we expect

- Genuine interest in, and basic knowledge of, cloud platforms, operations, networking, hardware and software
- Programming experience in at least one of Python, Go, C++ or Java
- Understanding of networking basics (Ethernet, IP, TCP/UDP, routing)
- Familiarity with Git
- Basic understanding of containers, Kubernetes and GitOps

## What we offer

Mentoring, work on production systems, an international team and a chance of a permanent role.
