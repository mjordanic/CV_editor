# Senior Data Engineer – Data Platform

**Company:** Halden Pay (fictional name; cross-border business payments)
**Location:** Lisbon, Portugal (hybrid, 3 days per week in the office)
**Team:** Data Platform

## The role

Halden Pay is growing quickly, and data sits at the centre of that plan. We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to join the Data Platform team. The team builds and runs the shared data platform that analysts, data scientists and product teams rely on, with a focus on reusable frameworks rather than one-off pipelines.

## Responsibilities

- Own the design, build and day-to-day running of the company's central data platform
- Evaluate and introduce new open-source tools where they bring a clear benefit
- Work with data scientists, analytics engineers and BI colleagues to turn their needs into platform features
- Define and enforce data quality standards, governance rules and access policies

## Tech stack

- Cloud: Google Cloud (BigQuery, Cloud Run, GKE)
- Orchestration and transformation: Airflow, dbt
- Engineering: Python, SQL, Docker, CI/CD, Git

## Requirements

- Experience designing, building and operating production data platforms or cloud data warehouses at scale
- Advanced Python and SQL, including query optimisation on large datasets
- Strong data modelling skills (dimensional modelling; graph modelling is a plus)
- Hands-on experience with Google Cloud or a comparable cloud (managed warehouse, serverless compute, containers)
- Curiosity about new data architecture ideas and open-source practices
- Good professional English; Portuguese is a plus

If you meet most but not all of the points above, you are still welcome to apply.

## Benefits

- Base salary plus annual bonus
- Training and certification budget
- Support for open-source contributions
- Hybrid work from a central Lisbon office
