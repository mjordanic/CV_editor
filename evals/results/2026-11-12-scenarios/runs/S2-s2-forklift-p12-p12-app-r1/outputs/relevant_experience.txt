PROJECT: Wedding and party catering (Mesnica Kod Ante, 2022-2025)
- Prepared whole lambs and suckling pigs for spit-roasting for 3 weddings and about 10 family parties a year; ordered, butchered, seasoned and delivered on the morning of the event.
- Largest order: 4 lambs and 2 pigs for a 180-guest wedding in Samobor, 2024.

PROJECT: Shop sausage range (Mesnica Kod Ante, 2020-present)
- Developed the shop's lamb merguez recipe in 2020 and adjusted the ćevapi mix to 70% beef / 30% lamb after customer feedback.
- The ćevapi won 2nd place at the Zagreb County craft meat fair (Sajam mesnih proizvoda), 2023.
- Make about 40 kg of fresh sausage a week in the busy season.

PROJECT: Dry-aged beef trial (Mesnica Kod Ante, 2024)
- Ran a 6-week trial dry-ageing beef ribs (21 and 28 days) in a cabinet the owner rented; kept weight-loss and temperature records.
- The shop now sells dry-aged rib on pre-order only, about 15 kg a month.

PROJECT: Stall closing routine (Tržnica Dolac meat hall, 2017)
- Wrote the stall's one-page closing and cleaning checklist (slicer, mincer, counter, floor drain) after a market hygiene inspection remark; it is still taped inside the cold room door.
