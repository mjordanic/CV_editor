# Backend Engineer (Python) - Shipment Tracking Platform

**Company:** Tessel Cargo Software (FICTIONAL)
**Location:** Antwerp, Belgium (hybrid, 2 days/week in office) – candidates based elsewhere in the EU may work remotely with quarterly on-site weeks
**Employment:** Full-time, permanent
**Team:** Routing & Tracking (7 engineers, 1 engineering manager, 1 product manager)

## About the role

Tessel Cargo Software builds the software that mid-sized freight forwarders use to plan, route and track shipments across Europe. Our Routing & Tracking team owns the services that turn thousands of carrier events per minute into a reliable live picture of where every container, trailer and parcel is.

We are looking for a Backend Engineer with a few years of solid production experience to help us grow this platform. You will work on event ingestion, route-optimisation APIs and the customer-facing tracking API, and you will be on the on-call rota (one week in six, supported by a runbook-driven team).

## What you will do

- Design, build and maintain Python (FastAPI) services that ingest carrier events from EDI, REST webhooks and message queues
- Own parts of our PostgreSQL data model and write migrations that are safe to run on live data
- Improve the performance and reliability of our public tracking API (currently ~400 requests/second at peak)
- Work with the data team to expose clean event streams (Kafka) for analytics and ETA prediction
- Write automated tests, review code and contribute to our CI/CD pipeline (GitHub Actions)
- Help investigate production incidents and write blameless post-mortems
- Take part in architecture discussions and document decisions in short design notes

## What you bring

**Must have**
- 3+ years of professional experience building backend services in Python (or a comparable language such as Go, Java or Kotlin, if you are happy to work mainly in Python)
- Solid experience with relational databases, preferably PostgreSQL, including query tuning and schema migrations
- Experience designing and consuming REST APIs
- Experience with Docker and deploying services to a cloud environment (we use AWS)
- Practical understanding of automated testing and CI/CD
- Good written and spoken English; you can explain technical trade-offs clearly

**Nice to have**
- Experience with Kafka or another event-streaming platform
- Experience with Kubernetes (we run on EKS) and infrastructure as code (Terraform)
- Exposure to observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry)
- Knowledge of logistics, transport or supply-chain domains, or EDI formats (EDIFACT, X12)
- Dutch or French language skills

## What we offer

- Salary range EUR 62,000 – 82,000 gross per year depending on experience
- 28 days of holiday, a learning budget of EUR 1,500 per year and a home-office allowance
- Pension scheme, public-transport card and a modern hybrid office near Antwerpen-Centraal
- A team that values pragmatic engineering, clear documentation and sustainable pace

Send us your CV and, if you like, a short cover letter or links to code you are proud of. We read every application and reply within two weeks. Our interviews are: a 30-minute intro call, a practical technical conversation about a past project and a short system-design discussion.
