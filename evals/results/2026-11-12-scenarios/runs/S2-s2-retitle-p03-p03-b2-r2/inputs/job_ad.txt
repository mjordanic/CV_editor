# Cloud Infrastructure / DevOps Contractor – Fleet Connectivity

**Company:** Corvale Mobility (fictional name; intercity rail and coach operator)
**Location:** Kraków, Poland (contract, remote-friendly; occasional office days)
**Engagement:** Service agreement (B2B / freelance), not an employment contract
**Rate:** 95 – 125 PLN per hour

## The engagement

Corvale is preparing a new generation of trains and needs a cloud infrastructure engineer for the team that links the vehicles to the company's cloud platform. You will help shape the architecture from the start and take it to production.

## Scope

- Design, build and operate the cloud infrastructure that links on-board train systems with backend services: telemetry ingestion, alerting, video streaming and remote content updates
- Maintain the infrastructure-as-code repository (Terraform with Terragrunt), keeping it tidy and approachable
- Lead technical proposals across backend and infrastructure from idea to production
- Contribute to system design and challenge decisions with arguments
- Set up secure train-to-cloud communication (mutual TLS, certificate management, network design)
- Agree interfaces and responsibilities with train manufacturers' engineers
- Support backend developers in deploying services that are easy to monitor and run
- Keep the platform observable, reliable and secure

## Requirements

- Hands-on infrastructure engineering and cloud environment design and operation
- AWS preferred; other major clouds acceptable
- Strong Terraform and Terragrunt
- Good understanding of systems architecture and system design
- Networking knowledge, including mTLS and SSL/TLS
- Practical security awareness
- Experience leading technical topics and bringing stakeholders along
- Strong analytical thinking and clear communication

**Nice to have:** IoT or automotive/remote-device integration, backend or full-stack background (JVM preferred), Linux and virtual machines.

## Working model

Independent contractor, invoicing monthly for agreed services.
